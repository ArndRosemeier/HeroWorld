# Graffiti

Decal images for the walls around the mad scientist's quarter
(`hw_gen::urban::edge::tags`, drawn by `dystopiaview.rs`). Made with OpenRouter's
`meta/muse-image` (2026-11-08) at 1:1 on white, scaled to 256 px, and the white keyed
out to alpha (un-premultiplied). Each prompt ended with "isolated on a plain pure white
background, flat spray paint graffiti, no wall, no shadow, centered, square".

| File | Prompt |
| --- | --- |
| skull.png | Graffiti of a grinning cartoon skull with a lightning bolt through it, acid green and black spray paint with drips, 1950s comic villain style |
| zap.png | Bubble letter graffiti tag reading ZAP!, purple and acid green with black outline and drips, retro comic style |
| coil.png | Black stencil graffiti of a Tesla coil emblem with the words OBEY THE COIL underneath, crude spray stencil |
| wild.png | Wildstyle graffiti tag, sharp arrows and interlocking letters, hot pink, toxic green and black, drips |
