What is HeroWorld?

The city

A town that lives without you

Every world number grows its own city, from a hamlet of 300 people to a megalopolis of two million. Old towns at the core, Googie diners and streamline towers further out, rivers and coasts, parks with real grass and trees.

People walk to work, cars drive their lanes, flying taxis cross the sky, and when the sun goes down the windows light up one by one. Almost everything breaks, and the town quietly repairs itself where nobody is looking.

Drag the handle to watch the street go from noon to night.

Places to visit

Rockets, reactors and a hall of heroes

Up at the airfield the liner Skylark comes in to its mast. Below the streets runs the Aether Tube, with mole folk markets, buried ruins and the Doctor's lab. Take a lift down and see how deep it goes.

Co-op

Better with friends

Host your world in one click and read your room code to friends. Up to 10 heroes share one town: split up across the city or team up on the colossus. Nobody dies here. Villains get cuffed and carried off by police drones, and friends revive each other.

Single player is just as complete: your hero and your city are saved on your own PC.

Get it

Three steps to your first rescue

  1. Download the launcher. One small file, HeroWorldLauncher.exe.
  2. Run it. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the launcher is new and not yet signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Press Install, then Play. The first download is about 500 MB. After that, updates bring only what changed.
What do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 and a graphics card with Vulkan or DirectX 12 (most cards from the last eight years). A gaming PC with a dedicated card plays best. A Mac version is planned.

What does "alpha" mean here?

The game is early and changes almost every night. Expect rough edges, missing pieces and the odd bug. Your hero is saved on your PC and carries over to new builds.

Where are my saves?

In %APPDATA%\HeroWorld. The launcher never touches them, and the game is installed separately in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\HeroWorld.

Can I skip the launcher?

Yes. Download the plain zip from the release page, unzip it and double-click heroworld.exe. If you later point the launcher at that folder, it keeps every file that is still current.

Does it cost anything?

No. HeroWorld is free.
