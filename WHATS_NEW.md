# What's new in HeroWorld

Newest first. Every build pushed to `main` adds 0.001 to the version (in
`VERSION`) and a few lines here, in plain words for players.

## 0.286 (2026-11-14)

- start.bat starts again on Windows (its lines ended the wrong way for Windows' command window, which cut a word in half).

## 0.285 (2026-11-14)

- Every roof is solid now. Pitched and hipped roofs, mansards, domes, the Googie swoop, the factory sawtooth, tower crowns, taxi pads, parapets and the corner towers' caps used to be only drawn: you and the cars could fly or walk right through them. Their solid shape follows what is drawn, slope by slope.
- A test draws every kind of roof and probes it from just under its surface, so a roof can't lose its collision again.

## 0.284 (2026-11-14)

- The balconies and bridges of a gang's den are no longer bare. Behind every balcony's rail the gang has piled sandbags, crates and oil drums to crouch behind, and their pin boards, safes and cabinets stand against the wall; bridges have sandbags along both rails.
- The walk along the rail stays free, so you (and the gang) can still run from one stair to the next.
- Sandbag walls stand on the hall floor of hideouts too, among the card tables and cots.
- The way into a case's building is easy to spot now: a glowing teal neon frame round its door and teal arrows on the pavement pointing at it, from the moment you take the case until it is done.
- No more townsfolk standing about inside gang hideouts. Whoever is in there is the gang.
- After a hideout is cleared the police come in varying numbers: sometimes nobody, up to three cruisers for a big gang. The officers no longer just stand at the door: they go inside, walk to where the gang held out and collect the evidence, then come out and fly off. When several cars come, one officer guards the door.
- Officers no longer walk on the spot.
- This changes what is inside the hideouts of every town. Everyone playing together needs this version.

## 0.283 (2026-11-14)

- Waking or sending home a Herobot is now H (F is the Grapple Swing).

## 0.282 (2026-11-14)

- Two Herobots stand on Hero Hall's plaza next to the town's heroes. Press H beside one to wake it: it joins your team as a real player, with your karma and level, a random origin and element, and powers it bought itself. It follows you, rushes to trouble and fights villains, helps downed heroes up, and when it is beaten the drone flies it to the hospital. Press H near it again to send it home.
- A Herobot takes one of the ten places in your world and makes the fights bigger, like a friend would. Handy for trying multiplayer alone.

## 0.281 (2026-11-14)

- The town has animals of its own now. Wibbets, big-eyed climbers the size of a cat with glowing feelers, live in small troops on the pavements: they trot along the kerb, run up lamp posts and cling there, hop onto benches, bins and hydrants, and sit up to look around.
- Stand still near a wibbet on the pavement and it may come over, sit up in front of you and have a good look before it goes back to its round. Walk into one and it hops out of your way.
- A few citizens have a pet drakelet: a little dragon with a collar that flies on its lead, ahead of its owner and round them when they stop.
- Driftbells, glowing bells with long trailing arms, float in twos and threes high over the wide streets.
- All of them come in three colours, and every player sees the same animals in the same places.

## 0.280 (2026-11-14)

- Team voice. When a friend is in your game, hold Z to talk: everyone on your team hears you, however far away they are, and a "so-and-so is talking" note shows at the left edge. Click the small Voice button in the chat window to turn voice off, pick another key, set the team volume, or mute a friend or turn them up or down. No microphone? The panel says so and the game carries on.

## 0.279 (2026-11-14)

- Glide is gone: it cost the same as Flight and did less. In its place in the Travel powers tree is **Grapple Swing**: hold F and a line hooks whatever you aim at, up to 45 m away (70 m at level 20), and pulls you in. Steer, swing round buildings, and let go at the top to fling yourself far. It costs no energy.
- If you had bought Glide, the karma you paid comes back the next time you play.

## 0.278 (2026-11-14)

- The green "Tube exit" stairs can be walked down again: an invisible floor lay over their holes in the street. A new test walks down every street stair in the town.

## 0.277 (2026-11-14)

- Arrange the screen your way. Escape menu, "Arrange the screen": drag the minimap, compass, power tray, wrist radio and police monitor wherever you like, and drag a piece's corner to make it bigger or smaller. Enter or the green tick finishes; the round red arrow puts everything back. Your layout is kept for next time.
- The chat, powers and police computer windows can be dragged too (hold Alt to get the pointer), and they remember where you left them.

## 0.276 (2026-11-14)

- The chat window has a Team tab. Click it, press Enter and type: everyone in your session reads it, however far away they are, and the tab shows how many new lines wait for you.

## 0.275 (2026-11-14)

- The mouse now looks around by itself, with the pointer hidden, like most action games: no right button needed. Hold Alt whenever you want the pointer back for a click.
- Windows, menus, the chat line, the map and the police computer free the pointer on their own and pin it again when you close them. Escape still opens the menu.
- Prefer the old way? Escape, Settings, Mouse: untick "Always look with the mouse", and hold the right button to look.

## 0.274 (2026-11-13)

- Augments go straight into the power tree: every power you own shows its three sockets inside its box, and you drag an augment from the bag right onto the power. Drop it on an augment already there to replace that one.
- The free powers (Strike, Super Jump, Sprint) are boxes in the powers window too, with their sockets.
- On a small screen the powers window scrolls instead of running off the bottom.

## 0.273 (2026-11-13)

- Gang hideouts are no longer a stack of look-alike floors. Most are now dens: one great hall through the whole height of the house, with balconies at its ends at every storey and in between, open stairs that climb along the walls from one balcony across to the next, and bridges over the drop.
- Every storey has its own height, from 4 to 7.5 m, and its own rooms: one or two, some two storeys high behind a wall of glass, some open to the hall like a box at the theatre.
- Dens are a storey higher than the houses they were, and no two are alike: the hall stands at the street, at the back or in the middle.
- The gang walks all of it: stairs, bridges and balconies.
- Police cruisers land far more often. In 0.272 most of them only hovered over the door, because in narrow lanes the officers had no allowed way to it; now the cars wait round the door as well.
- This changes what is inside the hideouts of every town. Everyone playing together needs this version.

## 0.272 (2026-11-13)

- The police have a car and officers. When a hideout case closes, a Sky Patrol cruiser comes down out of the sky with its siren going: a black and white flying car with twin fins, gold stars, chrome skids and a turning red beacon. It lands in the street by the door, and the cars wait for it.
- Its canopy swings up and two officers in navy uniforms, white gloves and peaked caps get out, stand guard either side of the door for a while, then get back in and fly off.
- The cruiser is solid: you walk round it. Where there is no room to land it hovers over the door instead.
- Everyone playing together needs this version.

## 0.271 (2026-11-13)

- Nothing new in play: a test switch (`HW_CARRY=1`) that makes every villain carry an augment, for looking at the gems and the drops.

## 0.270 (2026-11-13)

- Augments: loot for your powers. Some villains carry one, with a gem glinting over their head (copper, blue or gold for common, rare and relic); beat them and everyone who helped gets one. Every boss carries one, and a hideout bust ends with a find.
- An augment adds one effect to one power, for good: more damage, or something the power never did before, like a bolt that also holds, a punch that burns, a shield that throws blows back, a faster flight. There are 39 effects, each with its own icon.
- Every power has three sockets, for three different effects. Open the powers window (B), drag an augment from the bag onto a power's socket. The window says in numbers what it will do there. A new one in a full socket destroys the old one.
- Augments from tougher villains are stronger, so there is always a better one to find. Ones you don't want trade in for karma to spend on powers.
- Villains above level 4 are tougher than before, to keep up.
- Knockdowns can be partial now: a staggering blow floors a minion after a hit or two, a boss after several.
- Everyone playing together needs this version.

## 0.266 (2026-11-13)

- Hideouts are now the exception: one tenement in four, with boarded-up ground-floor windows. The other buildings are ordinary homes again.

## 0.265 (2026-11-13)

- Find the thing: its room's crew guards it. Once you take it the whole gang comes for you and a crew blocks the door: get out of the building with it.
- Arrest the boss: badly hurt, the boss makes a run for it and calls for help before turning to fight again.
- Hideouts come in three sizes (den, hideout, fortress), shown on Crime-o-Vision as little houses. Bigger ones and tougher gangs pay more karma; small ones pay less than before.
